# Data methodology

*Last verified: November 2026*

## Observances (`observances.js`)
**UN days.** Selected from the [UN list of international days and weeks](https://www.un.org/en/observances/list-days-weeks) for how often chapters build programs around them, SDG coverage across all 17 goals, and local relevance (cities, transit, disability, water, democracy). Official English and Spanish titles are quoted as published. Days that move each year (World Habitat Day, the first Monday of October; World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims, the third Sunday of November) use a `rule` instead of a fixed date.

Two anniversaries are included as historical facts rather than UN-proclaimed days: the signing of the UN Charter (June 26, 1945) and the adoption of the CRPD (December 13, 2006). United Nations Day (October 24) marks the Charter's entry into force in 1945.

The event ideas are original suggestions, not UN guidance.

**Chapter reminders.** Drawn from UNA-USA's public materials:
| Reminder | Source |
|---|---|
| Annual report due January 15 (community chapters) | Chapter bylaws based on UNA-USA's template, e.g. [UNA-Kansas City bylaws](https://www.una-kc.org/_files/ugd/26f5ef_dbb7f7376cfe43a79820cf657323be4f.pdf) |
| Form 990 to UNA-USA by May 15 | [Chapter Leader Update, April 2023](https://unausa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Preview-CLU-April23.pdf) |
| Book UN Day space and request a speaker in early September (campus) | [Fall Semester Checklist](https://unausa.org/fall-kick-off-checklist) |
| Plus One campaign, September 24 to October 24 | [2024 UN Day Toolkit](https://new.express.adobe.com/webpage/5cIuhMXntd0vm) |
| End-of-semester report by December 20 (campus) | [Fall Semester Checklist](https://unausa.org/fall-kick-off-checklist) |

These dates come from specific past years and **can change**. The app labels each one "Confirm this date with UNA-USA" and links its source. UNA-USA shares the authoritative Chapter Leader Guide with chapter leaders directly. UNA-USA's 2025 awards criteria ask campus chapters of excellence to hold a UN Day program ([source](https://unausa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/UNA-USA-2025-Awards-Program-Overview.pdf)), which is why UN Day is marked as the signature event.

## SDGs (`sdgs.js`)
Official short titles from [sdgs.un.org/goals](https://sdgs.un.org/goals), English and Spanish.

## Issues (`issues.js`)
Each issue cites real SDG target numbers, with the target text paraphrased into plain language. Each "UN connection" names a specific instrument:
- UN Charter, Article 1 (peace and security)
- Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 25 (housing, food)
- CRPD (adopted 2006), Articles 9 and 29
- CEDAW (adopted 1979)
- 1951 Refugee Convention and UNHCR
- General Assembly resolution 64/292 (2010), the human right to water and sanitation
- SDG targets 4.7, 11.2, 11.7

## What's deliberately left out
- **Current events and political status** (funding levels, withdrawals, the Secretary-General selection). They change fast and belong in a chapter's own briefing, not a planning tool's data. For context as of November 2026: the US announced in January 2026 that it would stop participating in or funding 31 UN entities, and the tenth Secretary-General is due to take office in January 2027. Check current sources before citing either.
- **Official logos and the SDG color wheel.** This is an independent tool.
- **Personal data.** Nothing a user enters leaves their browser.
